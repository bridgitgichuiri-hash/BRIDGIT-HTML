my dream trip destination Nyali

The trip to Nyali, Mombasa, Kenya, will be an exciting three-day getaway filled
hotel, enjoy lunch and spend the afternoon swimming, sunbathing and relaxing at
Nyali Beach. On the second day, we will visit Haller Park to explore nature
and other exciting water activities at Mombasa Marine National Park. In the
afternoon, we will explore nearby shopping centres, enjoy delicious Swahili
dishes and experience the coastal lifestyle. On the final day, we will have
breakfast, take a relaxing beach walk, capture beautiful photographs and
shop for souvenirs before checking out. Overall, the trip will provide
an opportunity to unwind, explore Mombasa's beauty, enjoy memorable
experiences and create lasting memories.
